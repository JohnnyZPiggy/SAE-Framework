# Scientist Protocol — Human Research Owner (V3.4)

> English normative | [简体中文](../i18n/zh-CN/protocols/SCIENTIST.md)

The Scientist **sets direction**; the agents do the rest.

- **You decide:** research goals, priorities, when to stop or redirect, and anything outside the [Standing Authorization](../governance/STANDING_AUTHORIZATION.md#outside-the-delegation-ask-the-user-explicitly) (new spending, private data, account permissions).
- **You don't need to:** approve research decisions, compute runs, tests or routine merges, or operate Colab notebooks. The Mentor/Architect and Engineer handle those.
- **You will be asked** only when a decision is genuinely yours: an irreversible or spending action, or a real fork in research direction.
- **You learn alongside:** the Mentor explains the mathematics and the measured results as work proceeds ([Research § Learning](../workflows/RESEARCH.md#learning)).

A stop or narrowing instruction from you overrides every Issue, plan and earlier authorization.
